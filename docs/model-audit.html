What is each method allowed to know?
Oracle OLS

Observes the unobserved latent scores in known population units. Uses an exact t critical value. This is a reference experiment, not an estimator available to the analyst.

Two-step, naive uncertainty

Estimates the measurement model, then the structural coefficients. Its plug-in standard error treats the estimated latent correlation matrix as if it were known.

Null-calibrated diagnostic

Uses the same two-step estimates, with a critical value learned from a separate simulated null distribution. Held-out null simulations check the resulting false-positive rate.

05 / WHAT THIS EXPERIMENT CAN SUPPORT

A simulation is a controlled world.
Keep its boundaries visible.

Generating model
Five Gaussian latent variables; four predictors with equal correlation .35; three reflective indicators per factor; independent measurement errors. Loadings .50, .75, and .90.
Two different truths
Under the alternative, the coefficient differences are +.15 or −.05. Under the null, the compared coefficients are equal. Independent streams supply calibration, null validation, and alternative evaluation.
Denominators
Primary rates use all attempted simulations; failed fits count as non-rejections. Fit validity, proper solutions, and convergence are reported separately. Ordering reversal among good fits has its own conditional denominator.
Uncertainty
95% pointwise Monte Carlo intervals. Wilson intervals for ordinary rates; two-sample resampling intervals for calibrated rates include uncertainty in the null critical value. No simultaneous confidence claim.
Outside the experiment
No observed workplace data, missingness, nonnormality, cross-loadings, or correlated measurement errors. Model fit does not establish causality. A simulation-specific cutoff is not a real-data inference procedure.
